## Discovery sources

- [Comparative toxicity, phytochemistry, and use of 53 Philippine medicinal plants](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8685920/): original laboratory study with Philippine ethnobotanical context. Its brine-shrimp assay does not establish safe human doses or clinical efficacy. Table 1 includes mayana and takip-kohol under source-used botanical names.
- [Quantitative ethnobotany in Loboc, Bohol](https://ethnobotanyjournal.org/index.php/era/article/view/7714): discovery lead for kalabo and Philippine community use; review the full paper before extracting claims.
- [PCHRD 1982 annual report](https://www.pchrd.dost.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/1982-Annual-Report.pdf): historical research lead for luya and luyang dilaw, not evidence of present-day clinical approval.
- [PCHRD 2015 annual report](https://www.pchrd.dost.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/AR2015EditedNov25.pdf): research lead for malunggay; research activity is not proof of treatment benefit.
- [NCCIH Aloe Vera](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/aloe-vera): safety/evidence review lead; separately establish Philippine relevance and identity.

Search-result discovery is not full-text verification. No preparation or dosage should be extracted from these leads without reviewing the exact source and population.

## Publication checklist for each candidate

1. Resolve identity and Philippine/local names using botanical and regional sources.
2. Read primary clinical studies where available; record population, formulation, comparison, outcomes, and limitations. Label traditional or preclinical evidence explicitly where human evidence is absent.
3. Independently verify safety. Do not supply a home dose when only a standardized research formulation was studied.
4. Obtain a taxon-matched, commercially reusable image; inspect it visually and store it locally with full attribution.
5. Store reproducible built-in content with stable identifiers, without overwriting community submissions. The current catalog is still database-backed; local image storage alone does not fulfill the planned file-based catalog architecture.
6. Have complete records reviewed before publication, test search/details/source links, and refresh AI embeddings after approved content changes.
