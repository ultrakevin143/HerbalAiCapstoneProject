# Herbal AI User Manual

## About Herbal AI

Herbal AI provides DOH-oriented information about Philippine medicinal plants, community discussion, and the Dr. Ai informational assistant. It does not diagnose illness, prescribe treatment, or replace a licensed healthcare professional.

## Browse the herb library

1. Open **Library** from the navigation bar.
2. Use search to find a herb by local name, Cebuano name, scientific name, or medicinal use.
3. Use filters to narrow results.
4. Open a herb card to view preparation instructions, dosage information, warnings, and DOH approval status.

## Create and manage an account

1. Select **Sign Up** and enter the required information.
2. Use a unique email address and a password of at least eight characters.
3. Check your email and complete verification before using restricted features.
4. Use **Forgot Password** to request a password-reset link. Never share that link.

## Dr. Ai assistant

1. Open **Dr. Ai Chat** and read the safety notice.
2. Ask a clear question about a listed Philippine herb, preparation, or traditional use.
3. Review the response and its safety guidance.

Do not use Dr. Ai for emergencies, diagnosis, prescription decisions, or to replace professional medical care.

## Submit a herb suggestion

1. Sign in and open **Suggest a Herb**.
2. Complete names, uses, preparation, dosage, warnings, and source information accurately.
3. Optionally upload a JPEG or PNG image within the stated size limit.
4. Submit the form. It remains **Pending** until an administrator reviews it.
5. Check notifications/email for the decision when those services are enabled.

## Community and messaging

- Use **Community** to create a thread, add replies, and engage respectfully.
- Use **Messenger** to send private messages to another user.
- Do not post personal medical, financial, or password information in a forum or message.

## Administrator guide

Administrators can review suggestions, manage herb entries and users, view statistics, and manage Dr. Ai knowledge-base entries from **Admin**. Verify sources and safety before publishing information, and review audit entries after each administrative action.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
|---|---|
| Cannot sign in | Confirm email/password, reset password if needed, and check verification status. |
| No verification/reset email | Check spam folder and ask an administrator to confirm mail configuration. |
| Dr. Ai does not respond | Refresh once, check your network, then report the time and prompt used. |
| Image upload fails | Use JPEG/PNG and reduce the file size. |
| Page/message does not update | Refresh, sign in again, and report browser/device/time. |
