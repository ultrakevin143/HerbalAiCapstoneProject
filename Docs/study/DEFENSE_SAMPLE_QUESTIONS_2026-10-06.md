## Questions 1–3

1. **“Bakit ninyo pinili ang topic na ito?”**
   Explain the observed problem and why studying it matters; interest alone is not enough.
2. **“Anong problema ang sinusubukang solusyonan ng system ninyo?”**
   Clearly connect the observed problem to the system's response: problem → solution.
3. **“Sino ang makikinabang sa system ninyo?”**
   Identify the target users and beneficiaries and explain how they benefit.

## Questions 4–6

4. **“Bakit ito ang methodology na ginamit ninyo?”**
   Explain why the methodology fits the research, not just its name.
5. **“Bakit ito ang technology na pinili ninyo?”**
   Explain the reasons for the programming language, framework and database choices.
6. **“Ano ang pinagkaiba ng system ninyo?”**
   Explain the specific contribution, approach or improvement rather than claiming that no similar system exists.

Original screenshot: [Questions 4–6](reference-images/questions-04-06.png).

## Questions 7–10

7. **“Ano ang limitations ng study ninyo?”**
   Know and explain the study's scope and limitations.
8. **“Paano ninyo napatunayang gumagana ang system?”**
   Explain testing, evaluation, validation and user feedback using actual evidence.
9. **“Paano kung hindi gumana ang proposed solution ninyo?”**
   Explain what the study's data and findings support and what they cannot establish.
10. **“Ano ang contribution ng study ninyo?”**
    Explain what the research contributes to the problem, users, organization or field.
